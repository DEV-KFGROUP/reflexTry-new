# ReflexTry — Content and Image Plan for All Pages

Prepared: 19 November 2026  
Scope: 90 pages — 48 tools, 21 other pages, and 21 articles.

This document contains suggested copy and creative direction only. It does not change the website. Article entries are editorial briefs, not drafted research conclusions.

## Core direction

**Try a clear challenge, understand your score, and work toward a personal best.**

Use plain, specific descriptions of what each tool does. Prioritize genuine gameplay captures, annotated examples, and real team photography. Keep important instructions in the webpage rather than embedding them inside images.

## Shared Structure for Every Tool Page

1. **How to play:** three short steps describing the actual controls.
2. **What your result means:** the unit, scoring rule, and whether higher or lower is better.
3. **Try this next:** one specific suggestion relevant to the task.
4. **Related challenge:** one closely connected tool, with a reason to try it.
5. **Common questions:** answers about that tool’s rules and behavior.

## Image Production Guidelines

- Capture the real tool interface at a readable size.
- Give each image one clear purpose: demonstrate a rule, show a game state, or explain a result.
- Use consistent thumbnail framing across the tool directory.
- Keep important labels readable on phones; crop around the meaningful action.
- Add explanatory arrows or highlights only when they clarify a real mechanic.
- Use actual recorded results for progress charts. Label illustrative examples as examples.
- Use authentic team photographs for About; do not invent portraits or testimonials.
- Keep essential information in HTML text, with useful alt text for supporting images.
- Avoid decorative stock brains, invented scientific imagery, and fake performance dashboards.

## Editorial and Publishing Notes

- Prefer task-specific language over promises about intelligence, clinical outcomes, or competitive success.
- Replace unsupported numerical improvement promises with the activities, practice focus, and a way for visitors to compare their own results.
- Keep names consistent across navigation, tool pages, category pages, and articles.
- Distinguish Grid Targets, Grid Speed, and Schulte Table instead of giving them interchangeable descriptions.
- Use these briefs for localized versions, translating instructions, units, examples, and image annotations together.
- Confirm feature, scoring, research, and data-handling claims against the actual service before publishing.
- Policy-page entries are content directions, not complete replacement legal policies.
